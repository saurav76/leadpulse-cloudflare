# LeadPulse — ethical high-volume growth playbook (no LinkedIn)

Updated 2026-11-12. Stage: preparation; **no messages sent** and **no customer acquisition claims**.

## Positioning
Target real businesses whose enquiry forms affect revenue: web agencies, SEO/performance teams, web-maintenance providers, freelancers maintaining multiple client websites, B2B service businesses. US/UK initially. The current product tests whether a configured form is **visibly present**, not whether a test lead reached an inbox; never imply otherwise.

## Channel priorities (excluding LinkedIn completely)
1. SEO pages and search ads only after a testable signup funnel and reviewed legal pages exist.
2. Community contributions (Reddit, developer/agency forums, X, relevant Facebook groups) where rules permit; prioritize answering questions with useful advice, not repetitive promotion. **No posts have been made.**
3. Website contact forms to reach agencies only when a business relationship or lawful basis for the outreach can be established; do not use contact forms to bypass spam protections.
4. Personalized, compliance-reviewed B2B cold email where permitted, from domain-authenticated marketing infrastructure **separate from transactional mail**.
5. Permission-based opt-in waitlists/referrals after the public signup and privacy pages are approved.

## Pipeline states
`researched → needs_review → eligible → approved_to_contact → sent → replied → trial_started → verified → onboarded → activated → retained`; special terminal states: `bounced`, `unsubscribed`, `suppressed`, `ineligible`.

Business website URLs and publicly listed generic business contact channels are preferred. Do not collect private addresses, generate guessed addresses, buy unverified lists, send to scraped random contacts, use fake accounts, evade rate limits, or post on LinkedIn.

Each contact must have source URL, country, why the product fits, contact channel, outreach eligibility, timestamp, status and suppression history. Deliverability must be verified; "publicly listed" does **not** mean "consented". UK PECR, GDPR, CAN-SPAM and platform terms require jurisdiction-specific checks; absence of consent does not automatically permit direct marketing.

## Channel tracking
Use explicit `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` values in trial links; the application stores only allowlisted lowercase identifiers (e.g. `?utm_source=reddit&utm_campaign=form_health_launch`). Do not claim attribution proves conversion causality. Track research, eligibility, sent, replied, registered, verified, website authorized, first healthy check, active monitoring and retained usage separately.

## Evidence-first growth targets
No hard customer cap. Volume is constrained by permission, relevance, monitoring quotas, provider deliverability and support capability, not indiscriminate email counts. Report actual customer activation weekly; review low-quality traffic and cohort retention before ramping contact volume.
